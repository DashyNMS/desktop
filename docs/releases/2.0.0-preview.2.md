A preview of DashyNMS v2.0.0, a native Windows application for LibreNMS. These notes cover changes since **2.0.0-preview.1**.

## New

- **Choose how Dashy replies.** Each chat can use **Conversational** for concise explanations or **Evidence based** for separate facts, counts and measurements. Choose a default under **Settings → Intelligence**; individual chats remember their own style.
- **Status inside the answer.** Verified device names, device states and alert severities appear directly in replies, with severity-coloured bullet points. Turn off **Colour status indicators** in Intelligence settings for neutral colours; the labels and information remain visible.
- **See what was checked.** Expand the activity pill above an answer to see completed lookups, their observation times and result summaries. New execution records are saved with the conversation; older answers are not given invented lookup history.
- **Recover an unfinished answer.** Failed, stopped and interrupted replies keep their available text and offer **Retry** or **Edit question**. Their outcome and submitted question are retained after a restart.

## Improvements

- **Chats organised by activity.** Pinned chats stay at the top, followed by Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week and Earlier this month, then older months and years. New activity moves a chat into the right group, and the headings update after midnight.
- **A clearer composer.** Model and Reply style have labels beneath the writing area, with Ask/Stop on the right and a reminder of the keyboard shortcuts. Both selectors are disabled while an answer is running.
- **Simpler chat navigation.** The chat list stays visible, the active chat's name appears in the conversation header, and renaming happens beside the chat itself. Chat previews show readable text instead of Markdown formatting.
- **A new Intelligence icon.** Route sparkle replaces the chat bubble and heartbeat, making Intelligence easier to distinguish from Help or support.
- **More focused replies.** Evidence based answers list individual facts and measurements. Both styles add recommendations when advice or diagnosis is requested, or a definite follow-up is necessary; material uncertainty appears in a subtle **Notes** footer.
- **Answer controls.** Replies show their time and completion state. Copy answer preserves Markdown and expands device and alert references into readable names and statuses, with **Copied** feedback.

## Fixed

- Reading an earlier answer is no longer interrupted by streaming text, expanding lookup details or Copy feedback. **Jump to latest** takes you back to the newest content.
- Context remains accessible in smaller windows through a drawer, rather than disappearing with its toggle.
- Acknowledged critical alerts keep their severity indicator, with **Acknowledged** shown separately.
- Spotlight cannot change while an answer is using the previous scope. Finish or stop the answer before changing it.
- Context identifies cached device data and alert refresh times, and shows **Showing 8 of N** when its alert list is capped.
- Stopped and interrupted answers are distinguished from completed answers, rather than retaining a success tick.
- The question box uses the app's themed right-click editing menu. Graphs can be opened full size with the keyboard.

---

**Privacy:** Intelligence sends your questions and the network information needed to answer them to the AI provider. Web search can also send search terms to external search providers. Review the options under **Settings → Intelligence** before enabling it.

**Installation:** Per-user install, no admin prompt. Upgrading from an earlier version carries your settings and sign-in over automatically.
