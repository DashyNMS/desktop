DashyNMS 2.0 is one app for **Windows, macOS and Linux**, with Intelligence, Network Search, independent sensor definitions and LibreNMS 26.10 and 26.11 support.

## New

- **One app on Windows, macOS and Linux.** Every page works the same on each system, with native notifications, start at sign-in and browser sign-in. Windows Setup, signed and notarised Mac disk images for Intel and Apple silicon, and Linux AppImages for x64 and ARM64. Installing over 1.x keeps your settings, sign-in and data.
- **Intelligence.** Ask about your network in plain words. It looks things up in LibreNMS, Graylog and Unimus, shows what it checked, and can only read. Gemini can use network results that Antigravity has shortened in the conversation. Before your first question it explains what's sent and to whom.
- **Choose who answers.** Use your own ChatGPT plan, or an AI agent on your computer: Codex, Claude Code, GitHub Copilot or Gemini. Agents run locked down, with only DashyNMS's read-only network tools. Pick the provider, model and reasoning for each chat, and move a chat between providers. Web search is available with supported providers. The picker tells you when a provider cannot search.
- **Answers you can use.** Graphs, device states and alert severities appear in the answer. Choose how much it says, from Brief to Full report, and give Dashy a personality. Export a chat as a PDF or Markdown.
- **Chats and Spotlight.** Keep and pin several chats, or use one temporary chat that's never saved. Spotlight keeps a chat to devices, ports, groups, locations or alerts, and **Ask Intelligence** starts one from almost anything you can right-click. In the context sidebar, mentioned devices show ignored, unknown and maintenance states.
- **Network Search.** Find an IP address, network, MAC address or name from one box. See the switch and port it plugs into, the routers that know it, its subnet and any conflicts, and which sources couldn't be read. Press Ctrl+Shift+F, or Cmd+Shift+F on a Mac.
- **Names and colours for sensor states.** Numeric states show meaningful labels, from definitions that update separately from the app.
- **Grouped alerts for LibreNMS 26.10.** A rule firing on eight cores is one row with eight faults. Counts, notifications, acknowledgement and history all follow the group.
- **Ports as cards or a table,** with one clear status for each port.
- **Device logos** from your LibreNMS server in Device Details.
- **Spotlight on log cards.** Keep a dashboard Event log or Graylog card to some devices, a group or a location.
- **Export logs to CSV,** from Graylog, event logs and alert history.
- **Auto-refresh for the event and alert logs,** keeping your filters and selection.
- **Update channels.** Choose Stable, Nightly or Edge in Settings › Updates, and whether updates download by themselves.
- **Sign DashyNMS Mobile in with a QR code** from Settings › Server.
- **Optional error and usage sharing.** Off until you choose it, each part separately, with nothing about your network in it.
- **Help, inside the app.** The user guide for the version you're running, with search, back and forward, and links between pages. Open it from the sidebar, or press F1 for help with the page you're on. It works offline, and from the sign-in window. Intelligence can answer how-to questions from it too.

## Changed

- **What's New** is one list for the whole version, bundled for offline use. Nightly builds show it as it grows, and Settings always has it.
- **Settings** has separate sections for app information, the server, updates and diagnostics, and **Apply** saves without closing.
- **Rules for LibreNMS 26.10 and 26.11.** The editor has settings for each entity, and flags rules that test columns the server has removed. DashyNMS never rewrites a rule for you.
- **Accounts with limited access.** Counts say what they cover, and anything this account can't see or acknowledge is named.
- **Log entries open where they belong,** with the entry selected, and more Graylog messages match their device.
- **Ports.** The table, cards, CSV and grouping use one status, and VLAN groups follow the VLANs a port carries.
- **Updates** choose the package for your system, check its SHA-256, and replace the app in place on macOS and with AppImages.
- **Unknown stays unknown.** Readings and states a server doesn't report are shown as unknown rather than zero, up or down.

## Fixed

- **Slow sensor lists** have longer to load and keep the last good readings, and missing readings show as unavailable.
- **VLANs in the MAC table** show on LibreNMS 26.11 and on older servers.
- **Neighbours for ping-only devices** no longer stay loading.
- **Recovered alerts** don't inflate current fault counts.
- **Alert colours.** The sidebar badge uses the worst severity among the alerts it counts.
- **Logs filters** show "All devices" and "Any type" when the tab opens, instead of blank boxes.
