A preview of DashyNMS v2.0.0, a native Windows application for LibreNMS. These notes cover everything new since **v1.1.0**.

## New

- **Intelligence.** Ask about your network in plain words: "What's alerting right now?", "How is the core switch doing?", "What changed overnight?". DashyNMS answers using your own ChatGPT plan, looking things up in LibreNMS, and in Graylog and Unimus where they're connected. It only reads: it can't change anything on your network.
  - **Continue with ChatGPT** signs in through your browser; DashyNMS never sees your ChatGPT password. It needs ChatGPT Plus or Pro and uses your plan's own allowance. **Manage usage** shows how much you've used.
  - **Answers show their working.** Expand the activity line above an answer to see what was looked up and when. Devices, device states and alert severities are marked in the answer itself.
  - **Graphs.** Ask for a graph and LibreNMS's graph appears in the answer. Click it to open it full size.
  - **Saved chats.** Keep several conversations, organised by when you last used them, and pin the ones you come back to. An answer that stopped or failed keeps what it had, with **Retry** and **Edit question**.
  - **Reply styles.** **Conversational** for a short explanation, or **Evidence based** for separate facts, counts and measurements. Choose a default in Settings; each chat remembers its own.
  - **Context and Spotlight.** A sidebar lists the devices the conversation mentions, their open alerts and what was looked at. **Spotlight** keeps a conversation to chosen devices, a device group or a location.
  - **Web search, with sources.** Intelligence can check current public information, such as firmware releases and vendor advisories, and cites where it came from. Turn it off under **Allow web search** in Settings.
  - **Settings, Intelligence.** Connect or disconnect ChatGPT, choose the default model and reply style, how much of a device's config the agent may see (a summary, the full config with secrets removed, or none), and whether to use web search. **Show Intelligence** turns the whole feature off.
  - Before your first question, DashyNMS explains what's sent to OpenAI. Passwords, tokens and SNMP communities are never sent.

## Improved

- **Log entries open where they belong.** Clicking an entry on the dashboard's Event log or Graylog widgets, or in Logs, opens that device's event log, Graylog or alerts with the entry selected and its details showing. A Graylog message from something LibreNMS doesn't monitor opens in Logs, Graylog.
- **More Graylog messages named.** A message's source now matches its device by sysName or short host name, not just the exact host name, so more messages show the device they came from.
- **Defaults in one place.** **Default map** now sits with **Tab to show at startup** and **Default log** in Settings, Window and start-up.

## Fixed

- **Neighbours for ping-only devices.** Device Details no longer stays loading for a device with no ports; it shows the switches that see it.
- **Logs filters.** The device and type filters show "All devices" and "Any type" when the tab opens, instead of blank boxes.

---

**Privacy:** Intelligence sends your questions, and the network information needed to answer them, to OpenAI. Web search can also send search terms to external search providers. Review the options under **Settings, Intelligence** before you use it.

**Installer:** `DashyNMS-Setup-2.0.0-preview.3.exe`. Per-user install, no admin prompt. Upgrading from an earlier version carries your settings and sign-in over automatically.
