A preview of DashyNMS v2.0.0 for **Windows, macOS and Linux**.

These notes cover changes since [preview 13](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/releases/tag/v2.0.0-preview.13).

## New

- **Cards or a table for ports.** In Settings, Devices, the new **Ports layout** choice sets how the Ports tab in Device Details shows a device's ports. Table is the default and shows every detail in columns you can sort. Cards show each port as a compact card, several to a row, with its name, status, speed, traffic, errors and VLANs. Search, Group by, export and the port graphs work the same in both layouts, and changing the setting updates an open Device Details window without a restart.
- **A clearer port status.** Ports now use one status rule in both layouts: green for Up, amber for Down, red for any port with errors (`Up · errors`, `Down · errors` or `Errors`), and grey for Disabled, Not monitored and Unknown. A reading that is missing says Unavailable rather than 0.
- **A fuller user guide.** The [guide](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/blob/master/docs/guide/README.md) now has how-to pages for the dashboard, devices, logs, maps and health, notifications, integrations, settings and maintenance, plus a troubleshooting page. Installation and reference pages are grouped separately.

## Changed

- **The Ports table matches the cards.** The Status column shows the same label as the cards instead of the raw interface state, and is a little wider. The CSV export's Status column says the same. Sorting by Status and grouping by Status now put ports with errors first, then Down, Up, Disabled, Not monitored and Unknown, and each condition has its own group.

## Fixed

- **Group by VLAN on the Ports tab.** A trunk no longer sits under VLAN 1 while its card lists other VLANs. Ports are now grouped by the VLANs they carry: **Trunk** first for ports that carry more than one VLAN, then one group per VLAN in number order, and **No VLAN** last.

## Faster

- **Ports cards on devices with many ports.** Only the cards on screen, plus a little either side, are built. Devices with hundreds of ports no longer stall when they refresh, when you search or when you change Group by. Tab and Shift+Tab still move from card to card in order.

## Trying this preview

Report issues through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/issues), including your operating system and, on Linux, your desktop environment. The Ports cards are new, so please say if one looks wrong in your theme or on a device with many ports.

Mac downloads use Developer ID signing and Apple notarisation. Windows distribution signing remains tracked in [#109](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/issues/109).
