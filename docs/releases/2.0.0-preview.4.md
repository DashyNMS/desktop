A preview of DashyNMS v2.0.0, a native Windows application for LibreNMS. These notes cover everything new since **v1.1.0**.

## New

- **Intelligence.** Ask about your network in plain words: "What's alerting right now?", "How is the core switch doing?", "What changed overnight?". DashyNMS answers using your own ChatGPT plan, looking things up in LibreNMS, and in Graylog and Unimus where they're connected. It only reads: it can't change anything on your network.
  - **Continue with ChatGPT** signs in through your browser; DashyNMS never sees your ChatGPT password. It needs ChatGPT Plus or Pro and uses your plan's own allowance. **Manage usage** shows how much you've used.
  - **Answers show their working.** Expand the activity line above an answer to see what was looked up and when. Devices, device states and alert severities are marked in the answer itself.
  - **Graphs.** Ask for a graph and LibreNMS's graph appears in the answer. Click it to open it full size.
  - **Choose how much it says.** **Detail** sets each chat to **Brief** (a sentence or two), **Balanced**, **Detailed** (one line per finding, with figures) or a **Full report**. Every level says what it looked at. Ask for more or less in a question and it follows that instead.
  - **Questions that need many lookups** get an answer. If one runs out of lookups, it answers from what it found and says what it couldn't check.
  - **Saved chats.** Keep several conversations, organised by when you last used them, and pin the ones you come back to. An answer that stopped or failed keeps what it had, with **Retry** and **Edit question**.
  - **Context and Spotlight.** A sidebar lists the devices the conversation mentions, their open alerts and what was looked at. **Spotlight** keeps a conversation to chosen devices, a device group or a location.
  - **Web search, with sources.** Intelligence can check current public information, such as firmware releases and vendor advisories, and cites where it came from. Turn it off under **Allow web search** in Settings.
  - **Settings, Intelligence.** Connect or disconnect ChatGPT, choose the default model and detail, how much of a device's config the agent may see (a summary, the full config with secrets removed, or none), and whether to use web search. **Show Intelligence** turns the whole feature off.
  - Before your first question, DashyNMS explains what's sent to OpenAI. Passwords, tokens and SNMP communities are never sent.
- **Grouped alerts for LibreNMS 26.10.** LibreNMS 26.10 can raise one fault per core, port, sensor or other entity a rule matches. DashyNMS shows them as one alert per device and rule.
  - **Alerts.** A rule firing on eight cores is one row, "8 faults", with an arrow that opens the list: what each fault is about, its state and when it started. "Partly acknowledged" shows when some are acknowledged and some aren't.
  - **Acknowledge** and **Return to active** act on the whole alert, and the message says what LibreNMS actually did. Each fault also has its own button, used when LibreNMS will act on that fault alone; otherwise DashyNMS says so and sends nothing.
  - **Details and history** show each fault's own details, and how often the alert has fired.
  - **The badge, tray, dashboard and Device Details** count and list alerts the same way, so eight busy cores count once.
  - **Notifications** come once per device and rule. More faults, or some clearing, update it quietly; recovery comes when the last fault clears.

## Improved

- **Rules for LibreNMS 26.10.** The rule editor has **Notify per entity** and **Max entities**, with a line explaining how they group faults. LibreNMS 26.10 removed four device columns (last ping, ping time taken, last poll attempted and agent uptime). Rules that use them show a **Review** badge and a review panel; new conditions don't offer them.
- **Accounts with limited access.** Alert counts say they cover what this account can see. An alert or device this account can't acknowledge is skipped and named, and a graph it can't see says so.
- **Device Details on LibreNMS 26.10.** A processor with no warning limit set no longer warns below 95%, as LibreNMS itself now treats it. The sysContact override, which 26.10 no longer reports, points to LibreNMS instead of guessing.
- **Log entries open where they belong.** Clicking an entry on the dashboard's Event log or Graylog widgets, or in Logs, opens that device's event log, Graylog or alerts with the entry selected and its details showing. A Graylog message from something LibreNMS doesn't monitor opens in Logs, Graylog.
- **More Graylog messages named.** A message's source now matches its device by sysName or short host name, not just the exact host name, so more messages show the device they came from.
- **Defaults in one place.** **Default map** now sits with **Tab to show at startup** and **Default log** in Settings, Window and start-up.

## Fixed

- **Neighbours for ping-only devices.** Device Details no longer stays loading for a device with no ports; it shows the switches that see it.
- **Logs filters.** The device and type filters show "All devices" and "Any type" when the tab opens, instead of blank boxes.

## Since preview 3

- **Detail** replaces the two reply styles. Conversational chats become **Balanced** and Evidence based ones **Detailed**.
- Intelligence counts LibreNMS 26.10 alerts and faults correctly, and uses an updated set of network tools.
- A question that needs many lookups now gets an answer instead of "Try asking something narrower".
- Saving chats no longer stutters the window while an answer streams, and one damaged chat file no longer locks the others.
- The notice before your first question now covers web search. If you accepted it before, you'll see it once more, and web search waits until you do.
- When the network tools can't start, the message says why: the server's certificate, the token, the connection or the settings.
- Grouped alerts, the rule editor's 26.10 settings and limited-access accounts are new in this preview.

---

**Privacy:** Intelligence sends your questions, and the network information needed to answer them, to OpenAI. Web search can also send search terms to external search providers. Review the options under **Settings, Intelligence** before you use it.

**Installer:** `DashyNMS-Setup-2.0.0-preview.4.exe`. Per-user install, no admin prompt. Upgrading from an earlier version carries your settings and sign-in over automatically.
