# DashyNMS user guide

<img src="https://dashynms.pckp.net/assets/img/favicon.svg" alt="DashyNMS" width="80" height="80">

**Your LibreNMS network, right on your desktop.**

Find an alert, check a device, follow its traffic and keep the information you need on your dashboard. This guide walks you through the jobs you do in DashyNMS.

[Website](https://dashynms.pckp.net/desktop/) · [Downloads](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/releases) · [Report a problem](https://github.com/DashyNMS/desktop/issues)

![DashyNMS dashboard with alerts, device status, pinned sensors and traffic](../screenshots/dashboard.png)

These pages cover DashyNMS 2.0 for Windows, macOS and Linux. While 2.0 is in preview, download a preview release to follow along. Some controls depend on your LibreNMS permissions and the data your server collects.

## Everyday tasks

- [Build your dashboard](how-to/dashboard.md): add widgets, pin sensors and focus log cards on a site.
- [Investigate and acknowledge alerts](how-to/alerts.md): find what fired, record a note and export a handover list.
- [Choose which alerts notify you](how-to/notifications.md): reduce noise and set quiet hours.
- [Investigate a device](how-to/devices.md): check ports, compare graphs and follow neighbours.
- [Schedule maintenance and rediscover devices](how-to/maintenance.md): plan work on one device or a selection.
- [Search and export logs](how-to/logs.md): trace events and save the entries you have loaded.
- [Use maps and health readings](how-to/maps-and-health.md): find related devices and check sensor limits.

## Set up your tools

- [Manage alert rules](how-to/alert-rules.md): review conditions and targets before changing shared alerting.
- [Connect Graylog and Unimus](how-to/integrations.md): add messages and configuration backups to device investigations.
- [Change your connection and desktop settings](how-to/settings.md): set a backup address, appearance and start-up behaviour.
